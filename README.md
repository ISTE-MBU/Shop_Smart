🛒 ShopSmart

AI-Powered Retail Intelligence Platform

ShopSmart is an intelligent retail management platform designed to help kirana stores and small retailers streamline inventory management, improve operational efficiency, and make data-driven business decisions. By combining AI-powered forecasting, offline-first architecture, customer commerce tools, and inventory automation, ShopSmart enables retailers to manage their businesses more effectively while enhancing customer experience.

---

📖 About The Project

ShopSmart is a smart retail intelligence platform featured by the ISTE Student Chapter, Mohan Babu University. The platform is built to address the challenges faced by small and medium retailers, including inventory mismanagement, stock shortages, product expiry losses, and limited customer engagement.

By integrating inventory management, predictive analytics, barcode billing, supplier management, customer loyalty programs, and multilingual support into a single ecosystem, ShopSmart helps retailers improve profitability, reduce wastage, and optimize day-to-day operations.

This repository contains the source code, supporting modules, and resources associated with the ShopSmart platform.

❓ Problem Statement

Small retail stores often depend on manual inventory tracking and reactive decision-making processes. This results in stock shortages, product expiry losses, poor visibility into inventory movement, inefficient supplier communication, and limited customer retention.

Additionally, many existing retail solutions are expensive, require continuous internet connectivity, and lack intelligent insights that help retailers make informed business decisions.

---

💡 Solution

ShopSmart transforms traditional retail operations into a data-driven ecosystem through a unified platform designed specifically for small and medium retailers.

The platform combines inventory automation, AI-powered forecasting, supplier management, barcode billing, customer engagement tools, and offline capabilities to improve operational efficiency and profitability.

📦 Smart Inventory Management

Track products, stock levels, expiry dates, categories, and supplier relationships in real time.

🤖 Predictive Intelligence

Analyze sales trends and inventory movement to forecast demand and recommend reorder actions.

🛒 Customer Commerce Portal

Enable customers to browse products, view offers, place orders, and access purchase history.

📡 Offline Operations

Continue critical retail operations without internet connectivity through local storage and automatic synchronization.
